# Basis demo video: voiceover script

Target: 2:30 total, narration about 2:25. One audio file per scene.

## 1. Title (0:00 to 0:06)
This is Basis. Fair value for Tessera's pre-IPO tokens, on Solana.

## 2. The problem (0:06 to 0:25)
Tessera's T-Tokens, like T-OpenAI, T-Kalshi and T-SpaceX, trade around the clock on Solana DEXs. But the DEX price is set by whoever is trading right now, and thin liquidity can pull it far from Tessera's own valuation. Tessera doesn't run a venue of its own, so nothing tells you if you're overpaying.

## 3. What Basis does (0:25 to 0:45)
Basis compares two prices for every token: Tessera's mark price, and the live DEX price from Jupiter. The gap between them is the spread. Below mark is a discount, a chance to buy under fair value. Above mark is a premium, a reason to wait, or to sell into it.

## 4. Dashboard Overview (0:45 to 1:10)
This is the dashboard, and every number on it is live. Each card shows the spread, the mark price and the DEX price, refreshed every thirty seconds. Right now all three tokens trade above their mark price. The chart tracks mark against DEX price over time, and live alerts flag any spread that crosses a key threshold. Every card also links straight to a trade, so you can act on a spread in one click.

## 5. Markets (1:10 to 1:30)
Markets lists every token side by side, with market cap, holders and the average spread. Open one, and you get its real trading history as candlesticks, straight from its main Meteora pool, all the way back to the first day it traded. Switch between six hours, a day, a week, or everything since launch.

## 6. Trade (1:30 to 1:55)
Trade is where the signal becomes an action. Pick a token, and the banner tells you where it stands. At a premium, Basis says so plainly instead of pushing you to buy. At a discount, it turns green. Choose Buy or Sell, pay with SOL, USDC or USDT, and get a live Jupiter quote with price impact before you sign.

## 7. Spread History and Alerts (1:55 to 2:10)
Spread History shows the highest, lowest and average spread for any window, with every raw snapshot underneath. Alerts brings each token's status into one place, filtered by severity and by token, so you always know which move matters.

## 8. Why it matters (2:10 to 2:24)
Every trade through Basis is real volume into T-Token pools, and every mispricing it surfaces pulls prices back toward fair value. It's built on Solana, where trading around the clock is fast and cheap.

## 9. End card (2:24 to 2:30)
Try it at basis-spread dot vercel dot app. Built for Stocklana.
